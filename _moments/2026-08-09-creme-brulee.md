My obsession for *crème brûlée* has reached a whole new level: I prepare the base at home, pack them tightly in my backpack with brown sugar and a blowtorch, bike through the city to see my friends, and unleash hellfire with an evil laugh to caramelize the top.

The result so good my friend used the word *obscene*. I couldn't agree more.
